- **Create and update form buttons name their action (`ViewCreate`, `ViewUpdate`, `DetailView`)**:
    - The primary button of a create form now reads "Create" and the primary button of an update form reads "Update", replacing "Submit", so a form save no longer reads like a workflow transition named Submit. The `submit-button` slot's `label` prop carries the same text; its name, its other props, and the button's submit, loading, and disabled behavior are unchanged.
      _Update tests or selectors that find these buttons by the text "Submit". A `submit-button` slot override that renders its own text is unaffected._
