- **Record name heading on read and update views**:
    - `ViewRead`, `ViewUpdate`, and `DetailView` now show a heading above the fields that names the record by its formatted name, and they request `formatted_name` when the view's field lists leave it out. The `record-name` slot and the `recordName` and `recordNameSkeleton` theme keys customize the heading. A serializer that shows `formatted_name` as a visible field now shows the name twice, and `style={"hidden": True}` on that field removes the field row.
