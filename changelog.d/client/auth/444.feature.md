- **Sign-in redirect toasts (`AuthorizingForm`)**:
    - The new `toasts` prop sets the text of the toast shown after the post-login redirect, as `success` when it arrives and `redirectFailed` when it does not. `ViewSignIn`, `ViewTwoFactorAuth`, and `ViewReauthenticate` pass it through. See [Build Auth Views](../../guides/build-auth-views.md#redirect-chain).
