- **`storeWorkflow` object state, object transitions, and model state fetches removed**:
    - `fetchObjectState`, `fetchObjectTransitions`, `fetchModelStates`, and `initializeObjectTransitions` are removed, along with the `objectStates`, `objectTransitions`, and `modelStates` caches. The `workflowObjectState`, `workflowObjectTransitions`, and `workflowStates` URL keys are removed too.
      _Read an object's state from its `workflow_state_code` and `workflow_state_name` fields, and its transitions from `valid_transitions`. After a transition, read both from the `executeTransition` response. `fetchModelStates` and the removed URL keys have no replacement._
