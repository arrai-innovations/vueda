- **`waitForCurrentUser` and `requireCurrentUser` replace `waitForInitialising` and `requireInitialized`**:
    - The guards wait for a current-user request in flight instead of sending a second one, and send a new request after a failed one. When the current-user request fails, `requireAuth`, `requireUnauth`, `requireRecentAuth`, `requireCurrentUser`, and `requireGroups` reject the navigation, and the next navigation asks the server again.
      _Rename imports and calls of `waitForInitialising` to `waitForCurrentUser`, and of `requireInitialized` to `requireCurrentUser`._
