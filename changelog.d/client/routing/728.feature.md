- **Models whose viewset sets `lookup_field`**:
    - Detail URLs and client routes, including the routes of a list selection, carry the value of `ModelInfo.lookupField`. The read, update, delete, action, and transition views load their rows by that value and send each row's primary key in bulk bodies, update bodies, and workflow requests, and the read and update views keep live updates. `WidgetReadOnly`, the read-only `WidgetCombobox`, `ColumnModelLink`, and `ColumnModelLinks` link an object, including a related object, to its read view by its lookup value.
