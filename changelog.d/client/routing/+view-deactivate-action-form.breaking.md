- **The stock `deactivate` route runs the action through `ModelActionForm` (`ViewDeactivate`)**:
    - `ViewDeactivate` rendered a self-service account card for every model: account suspension text, a field that required the user's own email address, and a "Deactivate account" button. It sent its own request, so a warning from the viewset showed as a 409 error and never opened the confirmation dialog. `ViewDeactivate` now works like `ViewActivate`: it lists the selected objects and confirms through `ModelActionForm`, with warning confirmation, bulk requests, and a "Deactivate {Model}" title. Both views share `ViewSelectedObjectsAction`. The default tone is `warning`.
      _The `consequences` prop, the `message` slot, the `success` event, and the `ViewDeactivate.message` and `ViewDeactivate.error` theme keys are gone. For a self-service account page, render `ViewDeactivate` with `confirm-text` set to the user's email, `action-verbose-name`, and your own content in the `confirm-message` slot._
