- **Serializer field subclasses use the stock field's mappings**:
    - A field whose serializer class subclasses a stock field now gets the stock field's widget, column, props, and initial value through `typeSerializerAncestors`, unless a mapping in any table names the subclass itself. An editable form no longer fails with "No widget component found" for such a field, and a reverse foreign key declared with a `ManyRelatedField` subclass renders read-only. Stock subclasses change too: lists show the email settings `preview_tag_data` field with `ColumnJson`, and an `EmailField`, `SlugField`, `URLField`, or `IPAddressField` over a model `CharField` or `TextField` gets the text input.
