- **`WidgetReadOnly` renders its prefix and suffix around a `link-item` slot override**:
    - A `link-item` slot override of `WidgetReadOnly` now replaces only the link. The widget's `prefix` and `suffix` props render around the override, as they render around the default link.
      _A project whose `link-item` override renders its own prefix or suffix can remove that text, or stop passing the `prefix` and `suffix` props._
