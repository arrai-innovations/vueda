- **`null` for an expanded single-object relation**:
    - A write that names a reverse one-to-one relation in `e` now accepts `null` and removes the saved child, by the same `on_delete` rules as a row left out of a list. A forward foreign key or one-to-one field with `null=True` accepts `null` through its expand as well, and clears the link. Before, the nested serializer rejected `null` with "This field may not be null.".
