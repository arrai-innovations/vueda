- **Expands of fields that a write may not set**:
    - A write that named a field in `e` could set it, even when `exclude_create_fields` or `exclude_update_fields` locked the field for that write, or its model field had `editable=False`. The expand now ignores the field's data on that write. `Meta.read_only_fields` still locks only the plain field, so a relation's rows can still be written through its expand.
      _If a form sets a locked field through its expand, remove the field from the exclusion list, or set `read_only: False` in the expand options of an `editable=False` field._
