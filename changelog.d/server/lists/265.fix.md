- **Search on a queryset that carries an aggregate**:
    - `VuedaSearchFilterBackend` now keeps the joins of a search field that reaches through a reverse foreign key or a many-to-many inside subqueries, and narrows the viewset's queryset to the matching objects. A queryset annotated with an aggregate such as `Sum` or `Count` now lists and counts correctly under a ranked (`V:`) search, which previously failed the request with `NotImplementedError: annotate() + distinct(fields) is not implemented`. Under a `#` or `~` search the same aggregate now counts each object's rows once, where the search's join used to multiply it. A queryset that filters on such an aggregate, such as `.filter(item_count__gte=3)` over `Count("cart_items")`, now keeps every object that matches the search.
    - A ranked (`V:`) search and a queryset filter through the same multi-valued relation are independent conditions, as they are under the other prefixes. A queryset filtered by `.filter(cart_items__quantity__gte=24)` keeps a cart holding one item with a quantity of 24 and another item that matches the search.
