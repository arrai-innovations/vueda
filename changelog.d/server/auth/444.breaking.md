- **Reauthentication for accounts with a two-factor device (`recent_auth_required`, `vueda.user/2fa/reauthenticate/`)**:
    - A user with a two-factor device must confirm a code within `ACCOUNT_REAUTHENTICATION_TIMEOUT` before calling `vueda.user/totpdevice/setup/`, `vueda.user/totpdevice/activate/`, `DELETE vueda.user/totpdevice/<pk>/`, or `vueda.user/2fa/recovery-codes/` (GET and POST); confirming the password no longer counts for them.
    - `vueda.user/2fa/reauthenticate/` confirms a TOTP or recovery code, and `vueda.user/totp_code/` also sends codes to a signed-in user. Activating a device counts as a second-factor confirmation.
      _Send users who have a device to a code form that posts to `2fa/reauthenticate/`; a password-only re-authentication view strands them._
