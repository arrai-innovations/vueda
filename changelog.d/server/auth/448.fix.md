- **Authenticator app devices (`vueda.user/totpdevice/activate/`, `DELETE vueda.user/totpdevice/<pk>/`)**:
    - An authenticator app added after an email or SMS device produced codes that sign-in rejected. An app added that way before this release keeps failing until the user removes it and sets it up again.
    - Adding or removing the app now gives the secret that all of the user's two-factor devices share a new value, so a removed app's codes no longer confirm sign-in, and email and SMS codes keep working.
