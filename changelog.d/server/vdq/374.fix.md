- **Queue items whose row another transaction holds (`send_message`, `send_email`, `send_sms`, `on_retry`, `on_failure`)**:
    - A step that found its queue item locked gave up. The item could then stay in `queued` or `sending` with no task, or lose the provider ID of a sent message. These steps now wait for the lock and try again, and `pull_sms_timeout_only` skips only the held item instead of ending its run.
