- **Nested expands under a viewset's own `Prefetch`**:
    - A nested expand now keeps its query plan when the viewset prefetches the relation with a `Prefetch` queryset of its own. A nested model with a `formatted_name_lookup_expression` returns its name instead of `null`, and the nested relation loads in one query instead of one query per row.
    - The viewset's `Prefetch` no longer needs `_base_manager` to avoid that `null`. [Query Cost of List and Retrieve Expansion](../../core-concepts/field-and-expand-semantics.md#query-cost-of-list-and-retrieve-expansion) describes how the plan places each join.
